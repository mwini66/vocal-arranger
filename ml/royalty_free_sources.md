# Royalty-Free Vocal Data Sources

This file tracks all sources, licenses, and download links for royalty-free vocal stems and songs used in the Vocal Arranger project.

## Genre Focus: R&B
We're focusing specifically on vocals to train more effective arrangement models that understand genre-specific patterns like:
- Smooth vocal runs and melismas
- Call-and-response patterns
- Verse-chorus-bridge structures
- Emotional progression typical in R&B

## R&B-Specific Search Strategies

### Freesound.org Search Queries:
- "R&B vocal" + "acapella"
- "soul singing" + "dry vocal"
- "neo soul" + "vocal phrase"
- "smooth vocal" + "melisma"
- "gospel vocal" + "runs"

### CCMixter Search Queries:
- Genre: R&B/Soul
- "acapella" in description
- "vocal only" tag
- Artists: Look for neo-soul and R&B artists

### Quality Criteria for R&B Samples:
- Clean, dry vocals (no heavy effects)
- Clear vocal phrases with distinct start/end points
- Variety in energy levels (soft verses, powerful choruses)
- Different vocal techniques (straight tone, runs, vibrato)
- Various song structures (verse, chorus, bridge, ad-libs)

## Target Sample Collection (20-30 samples):
- **5-7 Ballad vocals**: Slow, emotional, soft dynamics
- **5-7 Mid-tempo vocals**: Groove-based, moderate energy
- **5-7 Uptempo vocals**: Energetic, powerful delivery
- **3-5 Gospel-influenced**: Runs, melismas, call-response
- **3-5 Neo-soul**: Modern R&B with jazz influences

Fill in this table as you collect R&B samples. Prioritize variety in tempo, energy, and vocal style within the R&B genre.
